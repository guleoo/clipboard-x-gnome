## Fixes

- **Mouse selection ends on release**: fixed Word Selection mode remaining in range-selection mode
  after a single click. Range selection now requires holding the left mouse button and ends when
  it is released, including outside the panel. A single click only toggles the clicked word.
- **Dragging back shrinks the range**: dragging selects or deselects the range from the starting
  word to the current word, based on the starting word's original state. Moving back restores words
  outside the range to their previous state, including when dragging across rows.

Keyboard selection behavior is unchanged.
